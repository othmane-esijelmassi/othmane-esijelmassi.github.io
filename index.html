About

I am currently a Master's student in Quantitative Economics at the Faculty of Governance, Economics and Social Sciences (FGSES), Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), Morocco. I received my Bachelor's degree in Applied Economics from the same institution in 2026.

My academic interests lie in economic research, particularly in the use of economic theory and quantitative methods to better understand economic behavior and broader economic questions. My goal is to pursue advanced training in economics, with a particular focus on developing the theoretical and empirical skills needed for academic research.
